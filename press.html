SIMON LIBON  ·  GUITARIST / COMPOSER / PRODUCER  ·  LIÈGE, BELGIUM

BIOGRAPHY

Simon Libon is a Belgian guitarist and composer based in Liège, studying classical guitar at the Royal Conservatory of Liège. Alongside his classical background, he is developing an original contemporary instrumental project.

His music brings melodic electric guitar together with electronic production, exploring expressive textures, contrasts and virtuosic playing.

ARTISTIC PROJECT

Original guitar-led instrumental compositions are currently in development. Audio releases and further press materials will be added here when available.
