BIOGRAPHY

Simon Libon is a Belgian guitarist and composer based in Liège. Studying classical guitar at the Royal Conservatory of Liège, he brings a classical foundation to a contemporary instrumental project built around expressive electric guitar and electronic production.

His musical approach explores melody, contrast and detailed guitar textures, balancing intimate passages with virtuosic energy.

ARTISTIC PROJECT

Original guitar-led compositions combining melodic writing, modern production and electronic elements. New music is currently in development; official audio and release information will be added when available.
