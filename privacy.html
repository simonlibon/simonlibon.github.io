Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit simonlibon.github.io, contact me, or follow a purchase link to Gumroad.

Simon Libon is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests, contact simonlibon.music@gmail.com.

3. Why this information is used

Contact messages are used to respond to you. Technical information is used as needed to operate and secure the website.

This website does not currently use its own advertising or audience analytics tools. Third-party services linked from this site may use cookies or similar technologies. Any non-essential tracking requires appropriate consent where applicable. This setup should be reviewed if new embeds or analytics are added.

6. How long data is kept

Contact messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed for the correspondence or related legitimate purposes, subject to legal requirements.

7. Your rights

Where applicable under the GDPR, you can request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw your consent. Send requests to simonlibon.music@gmail.com. You may also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website develops, for example when music sales, merchandise or analytics become available. The latest version will be posted here.
